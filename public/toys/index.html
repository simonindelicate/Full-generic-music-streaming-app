Catalogue toys

Single-file front-ends, each built on the same open catalogue feed. None of them needs anything but the catalogue's address.

Players

  • SalonThe polished one: tinted by each record's artwork, with a full-screen listening room.→
  • RecordsA page for every record, with a gallery of the whole catalogue.→
  • SquelchLiquid, gooey and audio-reactive. Deliberately far too much.→
  • Squelch, mellowThe same, dialled down.→
  • Riso zinePhotocopier punk: two-colour risograph covers knocked about by the kick drum.→

Radio

  • Catalogue radioA continuous station with programmed segments and an optional AI presenter.→
  • Catalogue radio, slop editionThe same station, pointed at a different catalogue.→

Things to make and explore

  • MixtapeMake a C60 for someone and send it as a link. It arrives as a tape in a deck.→
  • Record TownA pixel-art town where every record is a building. Listen to earn the tolls.→
  • The Catalogue, chartedA night-time map explored by lantern, where songs play as you approach them.→
  • Into the sleeveEach cover turned into a 3D scene by a depth model running in your browser.→
