Your music, on a site of your own.

Drop your songs in and this page turns them into a small website that belongs to you: a page for every record, free listening for anyone who visits, and the radio, the mixtape maker and the other toys, all running from your own files. There are no accounts or subscriptions here, and nothing leaves your computer until you put it online yourself.

1

Who's it by?

The name visitors will see at the top of your site.

2

Your music

Each folder becomes a record. Songs on their own become one record you can name.

Drop songs, or whole folders of them, here

MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF or M4A. Put a picture in the folder and it becomes the cover. Anything that isn't an MP3 or M4A is turned into a good-quality MP3 for you.

Made your site here before? Start from it

Drop your site's zip file or folder onto the box above, then add your new songs. If you've lost the file, give the address instead and everything will be fetched from the site (that can take a while).

3

Make the site

Everything happens here in your browser. Songs that need converting take a few seconds each.
